Today, at the hospital, I saw a very strict and scary mother with her poor little girl. The mother yelled at the girl and slapped her just because she refused to have her eyes examined.
From what the mother said, I learned that the girl had accidentally hurt her eye, so she and her mother had come all the way from the suburbs to a hospital downtown. The mother kept emphasizing that they had traveled a long way to get there, and the girl's refusal to cooperate with the examination made her really angry.
From my perspective, the mother was very rude and unreasonable. Why couldn't she talk to her daughter more calmly and patiently? I understand that she might have been tired and worried about her daughter's injury, especially after traveling such a long way, but I still don't think that justified treating the child so harshly.
Fortunately, a kind nerse came over and omforted the girl. She tried hard to stop the mother from hurting her child and then talked to the girl patiently. She encouraged the girl to overcome her fear and face the examination bravely.
I felt sorry for the girl. She was probably already frightened by her injured eye and the unfamiliar hospital environment, and being yelled at by her mother only made things worse. Sometimes I think adults expect children to behave rationally when they themselves are too emotional to do the same.
